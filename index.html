Segment one · 10 to 40

From air to numbers

Sampling · quantisation · aliasing · formats

Four conversions

Pressure wave

Air moving. Continuous in time and in value.

Transducer

Already has a frequency response, a polar pattern, a noise floor.

Voltage

Continuous still, but now a proxy with a gain stage in front of it.

Numbers

Discrete in time, discrete in value. Both by choice.

Every step throws something away. The question is never whether — it is who decided what, and when.

Sampling — how often you look

44 100 times a second

Nyquist: you can only represent frequencies below half the sample rate. Above that line, sound does not become inaudible. It becomes something else — which is the next instrument but one.

Instrument 02 · The decimation ladder

Drag the rate. Listen to who disappears.

Ceiling 22 050 Hz. Without the anti-alias filter you hear both losses at once: what falls off the top, and what folds back down in its place.

The test signal is a synthesised soundscape — traffic rumble, wind, birdsong, keys. Load your own field recording with the file picker and the ladder runs on that instead.

If the city's monitoring network ran at 8 kHz, which species officially does not live here?

Nobody lied. Somebody chose a number, years ago, for reasons of storage.

Quantisation — how finely you look

Between the steps there is nothing

Sixteen bits gives 65 536 steps, eight gives 256, four gives 16. The error is not silence. It is distortion that follows the signal, so it sounds like a fault in the world rather than in the recording.

Instrument 03 · Bit depth, and why we add noise

Take it down to four bits, then switch dither on

65 536 levels. Dither adds triangular noise of about one step before quantising.

We add noise on purpose, so the error becomes weather instead of lies.

Instrument 04 · The tone that rises by falling

A sweep from 20 Hz to 20 kHz, sampled too slowly
Playing—
You hear—

The straight line is the frequency being generated. The folded line is the frequency that survives sampling. Where they part company, the sound goes down while the sweep goes up.

Segment two · 40 to 55

The spectrogram as a way of seeing

Time, frequency, and the choice between them

You cannot have both

Time resolution

When did it happen? Short windows answer this and blur the pitch.

Frequency resolution

What was it? Long windows answer this and smear the moment.

Instrument 05 · The window is the question

Same signal, different window

Window 46 ms · frequency resolution 21 Hz. Clap, whistle, or play the test signal and watch the same event become a smear, a shape, or a line.

The window decides whether this place is a state or an event.

Segment three · 65 to 85

Measuring versus listening

Decibels, weighting, standards, indices

Three steps away from the event

dB

A ratio, never a quantity. Always relative to a reference somebody picked.

A-weighting

A curve derived from 1930s experiments with quiet pure tones. Still the legal standard.

L(A)eq

One number for an hour. The average of everything that happened.

Each step discards more of the event and keeps more of the administration. By the third one, a scream and a fridge can produce the same figure.

Soundscape is not noise

ISO 12913-1 defines soundscape as the acoustic environment as perceived or experienced, in context.

The standard exists because measurement alone kept giving the wrong answer. A quiet street can be unpleasant. A loud market can be loved. Context is not a softening word here — it is the object of study.

One number for a forest

ACI

Acoustic complexity — how much the spectrum keeps changing.

NDSI

Biophony against anthropophony, as a ratio of two bands.

BI

Bioacoustic index — energy in the band where birds usually sing.

scikit-maad computes all of these in a line of Python. Each one compresses a habitat to a scalar, and each one hides a decision about which band is life and which band is machine.

Segment five · 100 to 115

When the machine generates instead of records

Koray Tahiroğlu and the SOPI group, Aalto University

From sampling a space to navigating one

GANSynth

Learns timbre as vectors in a 512-dimensional latent space. On its own you can only roll dice in it.

GANSpaceSynth

Runs PCA over that space so the axes mean something. Now a musician can steer instead of sample.

Tahiroğlu, Kastemaa and Koli, AIMC 2021. Open source — but it needs training and a GPU, so this is a demo, not a workshop task.

GANSpaceSynth paper Code SOPI group

An instrument designed against mastery

Al-terity is deformable and 3D-printed, with pressure and bend sensors driving that latent space in real time. When AI-terity 2.0 notices you have settled, it jumps to a new point in the space. The performer is held in continuous uncertainty.

Al-terity, NIME 2020 AI-terity 2.0, NIME 2021

A latent space is a map you can walk. Who drew its axes?

PCA takes them from the training set. The training set is the cartographer — and nobody asked it for a legend.

The same move, five times

  • Sample rate decides who exists.
  • Weighting decides whose discomfort counts.
  • Window length decides whether a place is a state or an event.
  • The pin decides a site has one sound.
  • The training set decides what can be imagined.

None of these are technical details. All of them were settled before anyone listened.
