Same two seconds, three ways of looking

Wave, spectrum, spectrogram

The wave

Amplitude against time. Tells you when something happened and how hard. Says almost nothing about what it was.

One FFT frame

Energy against frequency, for a single slice of time. Tells you what was there. Throws away when.

The spectrogram

One FFT frame per column, stacked along time. Frequency up, time across, energy as colour. Both answers at once — for a price, which is the next segment but one.

All three are the same numbers. None of them is the sound.

Segment one · 12 to 44

From air to numbers

Sampling · quantisation · aliasing · formats

Four conversions

01 · airA pressure wave. Continuous in time, continuous in value, and already shaped by the room it crossed to reach you.
02 · capsuleThe diaphragm answers some frequencies more loudly than others, listens harder in one direction than another, and hisses on its own. Three arguments before any electronics.
03 · voltageStill continuous, but now a proxy — with a gain stage in front of it that somebody set, and a noise floor underneath it that nobody chose.
04 · numbersDiscrete in time (how often you look) and discrete in value (how finely). Both are settings in a menu.

Every step throws something away. The question is never whether — it is who decided what, and when.

Sampling — how often you look

44 100 times a second

Now two milliseconds of a steady 1 kHz tone, looked at less and less often. The tone does not change. Only the number of moments at which we keep any record of it.

Nyquist: you can only represent frequencies below half the sample rate. Above that line sound does not become inaudible — it becomes something else, which is instrument 04.

Quantisation — how finely you look

Between the steps there is nothing

Sixteen bits gives 65 536 steps, eight gives 256, four gives 16. The error is not silence. It is distortion that follows the signal, so it sounds like a fault in the world rather than in the recording.

The theory under instrument 04

Nyquist, and the ghost that descends

“If a function contains no frequencies higher than B hertz, then it can be completely determined from its sampled values at a sequence of points spaced less than 1/(2B) seconds apart.” — Shannon, Communication in the presence of noise, 1949

fN = fs / 2

Nyquist frequency — a property of the recorder: half the sample rate. 44 100 Hz in gives a ceiling of 22 050 Hz.

fs > 2B

Nyquist rate — a property of the signal: twice its highest frequency. Strictly greater, not equal; a sine at exactly fs/2 can be sampled entirely at its zero crossings and vanish.

falias = | f − fs · round(f / fs) |

And for the common case fN < f < fs, simply falias = fs − f. As the real tone rises, its ghost descends.

What is actually in the file

A WAV is the numbers, written down

measureAt each tick, the voltage as a fraction of full scale — what the converter actually read.
roundMultiply by 32 767 and round to the nearest whole number. Sixteen bits, signed: −32 768 to +32 767.
storeWrite that integer as two bytes, low byte first. That pair is the sample. Nothing else happens to it.
header44 bytes in front: RIFF, the file size, WAVE, a fmt  chunk giving channels, sample rate and bit depth, then data and a length.
thenEvery sample, in order, to the end of the file. No compression, no model of a listener, no cleverness — which is why it is big: 44 100 × 2 bytes × 2 channels ≈ 10 MB a minute.
soA WAV can be read with a ruler. Open one in a hex editor and the sound is right there, in order, in plain sight.

Everything a WAV knows, it knows forty-four thousand times a second — and it never guesses.

MP3 does not model sound. It models a listener.

Why MP3 is allowed to throw things away

One sound standing in front of another

the membraneInside the cochlea, the basilar membrane is a strip that is stiff at one end and slack at the other, so each frequency sets a different place moving hardest. Hearing a pitch means noticing where the ripple peaked.
the consequenceA peak is not a point. It is a patch. Two tones close in frequency shake overlapping stretches of the same membrane, and the quieter one never gets a region of its own to be heard in.
maskingSo a loud tone drags a threshold up around itself — steeply on the low side, gently on the high side. Anything underneath that curve is inaudible while the loud tone lasts. Not quiet. Inaudible.
the encoderAn MP3 encoder computes that curve for every frame and spends its bits only on what pokes above it. What falls beneath is not compressed — it is not written at all.
andMasking also reaches forward and backward in time by a few milliseconds, which is why a quiet detail just before a loud transient can vanish too.

The file does not contain the sound. It contains what a model of you would have noticed.

Left: the sum so far, against the square wave it is trying to be. Right: which harmonics are switched on. At one sine it is a sine — the square wave has not appeared yet because nothing but the fundamental is present.

x(t) = (4/π) · [ sin ωt + ⅓ sin 3ωt + ⅕ sin 5ωt + ⅐ sin 7ωt + … ]

Odd harmonics only, each quieter than the last in exact proportion. Fourier's claim, from 1807: this works for any repeating signal. A square wave's corners need infinitely many, which is why nothing real has ever produced one.

Every sound in this lecture is a sum of sine waves. The only question is how many you kept.

Time resolution

When did it happen? Short windows answer this and blur the pitch.

Frequency resolution

What was it? Long windows answer this and smear the moment.

Before the spectrogram, one frame of it

What a frequency view actually is

inN consecutive samples — one short window cut out of the recording.
outN/2 + 1 numbers. Not frequencies: bands, called bins, each one the energy the window found in a slice of the spectrum.

Δf = fs / N bin width

twin = N / fs window length

At 48 kHz with N = 2048: bins 23.4 Hz apart, from a 43 ms slice of time. Make N bigger and the bins get finer while the slice gets longer. That single trade is the whole of the next instrument.

windowYou cannot just cut. A rectangular cut has edges, and edges are clicks, and clicks are broadband — so the slice is faded in and out first, usually with a Hann curve. The deck's instruments use Hann.
discardedThe transform returns magnitude and phase. Every spectrogram you have ever seen throws the phase away and draws the magnitude. Remember this — in the last segment it is the reason a neural network finds audio hard.
stackedSlide the window along by a hop, transform again, draw the result as a column. A spectrogram is that, a few hundred times over.

A spectrogram is not a picture of a sound. It is a picture of several hundred decisions about where to look.

Segment three · 72 to 92

Measuring versus listening

Decibels, weighting, standards, indices

Step one

A decibel is a ratio, never a quantity

L = 10 · log10( P / P0 ) power

L = 20 · log10( p / p0 ) pressure

Twenty, not ten, because intensity goes as the square of pressure — so log(p²) = 2 log(p). The factor of two is physics, not convention.

p020 µPa in air, chosen as roughly the quietest 1 kHz tone a good ear can detect. Every dB SPL you have ever read is counted up from there.
ChangePowerPressureWhat it is
+3 dB× 2× 1.41Twice the energy. Two identical machines instead of one.
+6 dB× 4× 2Twice the pressure. Turning one signal up by double.
+10 dB× 10× 3.16Roughly twice as loud, to a listener.
−3 dB÷ 2÷ 1.41Half the energy — and only just audible as a change.

Three decibels is a doubling. It is also almost nothing to hear. Both of those are true and they are why the number misleads.

Step two

A curve from 1933, still the law

originFletcher and Munson, 1933: the first equal-loudness contours — which sound pressure at each frequency is judged as loud as a 1 kHz reference. A-weighting is essentially the inverse of the 40-phon contour, the one measured with quiet pure tones.
nowDefined as an analytic filter by IEC 61672-1:2013, with class 1 and class 2 tolerances. C rolls off only at the extremes and is used for peaks. Z is defined flatness, ±1.5 dB from 10 Hz to 20 kHz — before it existed, "unweighted" meant whatever the manufacturer felt like.
sinceISO 226 revised the contours in 2003 and again in 2023. Awkwardly, A-weighting now matches the updated 60-phon contour better than the 40-phon one it was derived from.

be fairFor ordinary broadband noise — offices, traffic at a distance, a busy room — A-weighting tracks human response about as well as far more elaborate measures. It is not useless.
be preciseIt is wrong about exactly the cases that reach a tribunal: low-frequency plant, tonal hums, impulsive noise. The failure is specific rather than general, which is what makes it hard to dislodge.
in the fieldMeasure both and subtract: a large LC − LA difference is the standard screening test for a low-frequency problem the A-weighted number is hiding.

Step three

One number for an hour

LAeq,T = 10 · log10 [ (1/T) ∫ pA²(t) dt / p0² ]

The equivalent continuous level: the steady sound that would have carried the same total energy over the period T. Note that T is part of the name — L(A)eq without a stated period is not a measurement, it is a rumour.

What it keeps

Total A-weighted acoustic energy between two moments in time.

What it discards

All temporal structure. When it happened, how often, how suddenly, whether it woke anyone.

Consequence

A scream and a fridge can produce the same figure over the same hour. So Lmax, L90, SEL and a whole ISO specification for impulsiveness exist to put back what the average removed.

Three steps: a ratio to a reference somebody picked, a curve from experiments on quiet tones, an average over a period somebody chose. Each one discards more of the event and keeps more of the administration.

One number for a forest

Ecoacoustics needs to compare ten thousand hours of recording, so it compresses each one to a scalar. Three indices do most of the work. Every one of them starts by drawing lines across the spectrum and naming the regions.

ACI

How much the spectrum keeps changing, relative to how loud it is. Modulated sound scores high, steady sound scores low.

NDSI

Life against machinery, as a contrast between two bands. Runs from −1 to +1.

BI

How much energy sits in the band where birds usually sing, and across how much of it.

Each hides a decision about which band is life and which band is machine.

scikit-maad worked example AudioMoth

Index one

ACI · acoustic complexity

dk = | Ik − Ik+1 |

ACIj = Σ dk ⁄ Σ Ik

ACItot = Σbins Σj ACIj

I is the intensity of one cell of the spectrogram. Take the difference between each moment and the next within a single frequency bin, sum those differences over a short cluster of time, then divide by the total intensity in the same cluster. Sum over clusters, sum over bins.

the pointDividing by total intensity is what makes it an index of change rather than of loudness. A motor is loud and constant: big denominator, tiny numerator, low score. Birdsong is modulated: high score. It was built to infer how much singing was going on.
hidesThe authors said themselves it is for hi-fi soundscapes, that wind produces false positives, and that it is sensitive to how close the source was — the same bird nearer the microphone scores higher. It also scores human voices and cicadas as complexity, because it cannot tell what is singing.
worseIt accumulates, so the value grows with recording length and shifts with the cluster size j. Values are not comparable across durations or settings. And of the common indices it is the one most distorted by a difference in sample rate between two recorders.

Pieretti, Farina & Morri, Ecological Indicators 11 (2011) · maad.features.acoustic_complexity_index · soundecology::acoustic_complexity

Index two

NDSI · life over machinery

NDSI = ( β − α ) ⁄ ( β + α )

α = power in 1–2 kHz, declared anthropophony.
β = power in 2–11 kHz, declared biophony.
Output runs from −1, all machine, to +1, all life.

shapeIt is a normalised difference — structurally the same formula as NDVI, the vegetation index that reads greenness from satellite bands. Remote sensing, pointed at the air.
hidesThe bands are the argument. Anything singing below 2 kHz — bitterns, many frogs, plenty of insects — is counted as traffic. Any mechanical noise above 2 kHz is counted as wildlife.
rainOf the common indices this is the one most damaged by weather. Rain is broadband and lands squarely in the biophony band, so a downpour reads as a thriving forest. In one Colombian study of 18 336 minutes, rain-contaminated files had to be excluded before NDSI meant anything at all.
and yetBecause it is a ratio, it cancels absolute gain — so it is unusually robust to differences between recorders. Fragile about the sky, sturdy about the hardware. The opposite of ACI.
carefulscikit-maad's defaults are not these bands — it ships 0–1 kHz and 1–10 kHz. The same recording gives a different NDSI in Python and in R unless you pass the bands explicitly.

Kasten, Gage, Fox & Joo, Ecological Informatics 12 (2012), Remote Environmental Assessment Laboratory · maad.features.soundscape_index · soundecology::ndsi

Index three

BI · the bird band, by area

BI = ∫ ( S̄(f) − min S̄ ) df

Take the mean spectrum across the whole recording in dB. Keep the band 2–8 kHz. Find the quietest value in that band and subtract it, so the curve rests on zero. Then measure the area under what is left.

readsBoth amplitude and occupied bandwidth at once — a loud narrow whistle and a quiet broad chorus can land in the same place.
hidesIt has no upper bound and no natural zero, so a single BI value means nothing on its own. It is only readable as a comparison inside one consistent pipeline, and unlike NDSI it is not gain-normalised.
the scandalThe R implementation's own documentation states that parts were changed, "in particular log math, so this won't be directly comparable to the original code in the paper". scikit-maad's example page prints, for the same audio file: BI soundecology 52.84, BI MAAD 17.05.
so"The Bioacoustic Index" is at least three different numbers depending on which toolchain computed it, and papers routinely report it without saying which.

52.84 and 17.05 are the same forest, measured by the same index, on the same file.

Boelman, Asner, Hart & Martin, Ecological Applications 17 (2007) · maad.features.bioacoustics_index · soundecology::bioacoustic_index

Segment five · 104 to 116

When the machine generates instead of records

Koray Tahiroğlu and the SOPI group, Aalto University

Engel and others, ICLR 2019 · Google Magenta

GANSynth, in one slide

trained onNSynth — 305 979 single musical notes from 1 006 instruments, four seconds each at 16 kHz. GANSynth used a filtered subset: 70 379 acoustic notes between MIDI 24 and 84.
does notgenerate waveform samples. It generates a picture: a log-magnitude spectrogram, using a progressive GAN of the kind built for faces.
the trickA spectrogram alone cannot be turned back into sound, because the phase is missing — and phase is chaotic to predict. So it also generates the instantaneous frequency: the phase unwrapped and differenced, which for a held harmonic is nearly constant and therefore something a convolutional network can actually produce coherently.
the spacez is 256-dimensional, drawn from a Gaussian. Pitch arrives separately as a 61-way one-hot vector, and the discriminator is made to guess the pitch — which is what forces pitch and timbre apart, so you can hold a timbre and walk up five octaves.
speedOne forward pass, not sample-by-sample. Four seconds of audio in about 20 milliseconds, against roughly 18 minutes for the WaveNet baseline on the same hardware. About fifty thousand times faster.
cannotFour-second single notes of pitched acoustic instruments, and nothing else. No environments, no speech, no structure longer than a note. The authors say so themselves.

It learned what an instrument sounds like by learning what a spectrogram looks like.

From sampling a space to navigating one

The problem

256 dimensions with no names. You can roll dice in it, or blindly interpolate between two dice rolls. You cannot ask it to be brighter.

GANSpaceSynth

Runs PCA over the activations of an early layer of the trained generator, and hands back a few axes that do something audible. In their model PC1 turned out to control brightness and PC2 loudness.

What that buys

Two or three knobs instead of a black box — so a musician steers rather than samples. It runs inside Pure Data, and ships Hallu for placing points in the space and stitching the results together.

Tahiroğlu, Kastemaa and Koli, AIMC 2021, Aalto University. Open source under GPL-2.0, last touched in 2021 — it needs training and a GPU, so this is a demo, not a workshop task.

Where the idea comes from

A hundred and twenty years of hidden variables

Latent, from Latin latēre — to lie hidden. It arrives in machine learning already carrying a method: something you cannot measure, inferred from the pattern of what you can.

WhenWhoWhat “latent” meant there
1901 · 1933Pearson, HotellingPrincipal components: derived axes, ordered by the variance each explains. The direct ancestor of GANSpaceSynth's sliders.
1904SpearmanAn unobserved common factor behind correlated test scores. Latent means inferred, never measured.
1990Deerwester et al.Latent semantic analysis — the first time latent names a space of meaning, with words and documents both in it.
2013Mikolov et al.word2vec: direction in the space turns out to carry meaning.

Hidden cause, then best-fitting axis, then space of meaning, then a space you can walk — and finally one you can play.

A latent space is a map you can walk. Who drew its axes?

PCA takes them from the training set. The training set is the cartographer — and nobody asked it for a legend.

The same move, five times

  • Sample rate decides who exists.
  • Weighting decides whose discomfort counts.
  • Window length decides whether a place is a state or an event.
  • The pin decides a site has one sound.
  • The training set decides what can be imagined.

None of these are technical details. All of them were settled before anyone listened.

Next session

Four hours indoors, with what you recorded

  • Record before you come. One site, three microphone positions, a few minutes each.
  • Only your own recordings. Nothing from a sample library.
  • Bring a laptop with the files on it, not only in the cloud, and headphones.

There is no fieldwork on the day and no time to make good on a missing folder: everyone needs sound loaded within the first fifteen minutes or they work from the fallback pack instead of from a place they chose. Then an hour putting your own takes through the instruments from this lecture, Strudel taught from zero, and a choice between composing sixty seconds from your recordings or sonifying the sensor logs from the Nano boards.

This deck stays online and every instrument in it takes your files through the picker. Running them on your own material beforehand is the head start.
