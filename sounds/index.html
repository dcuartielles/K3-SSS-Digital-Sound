Fallback sound pack

Three sites around campus, three takes at each — close to the ground, at waist height, held up and away. Recorded for students who arrive at the workshop without their own material.

Open the sound pack on Google Drive Download the whole pack (zip)

The whole pack as one zip is about 2 GB, so it is not offered online. Open the Drive folder and download only the site folders or clips you need — no account required. The complete pack is on a USB stick in the room.

Already cut to clips: mono, 2 to 12 seconds, faded at both ends and level-matched. Put the clips in a soundwalk folder on your laptop and import that folder straight into Strudel — sounds, import-sounds, import sounds folder.

Using it honestly

Working from someone else's recordings is a legitimate position. It is just a different one, so say so in your share-out — and when you get to the third question, what did you lose that you wish you had kept, notice that you were not the one who decided what to keep in the first place.
